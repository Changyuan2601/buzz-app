# Messages navigation

Lists your channels, chats and DMs in the sidebar, below the navigation at the top of the left pane.

## Getting started

1. Choose **Messages** at the top of the sidebar.
2. Use **All**, **Solo** or **Team** to choose which conversations are listed.
3. Choose another view at the top of the sidebar, such as one a plugin adds, to list messages another way. Your open conversation stays open.
